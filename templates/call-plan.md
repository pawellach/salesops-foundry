## Key questions (5–7)

1. _Open question — establish context_
2. _Explore pain — "What happens when…?"_
3. _Probe impact — "What does that cost you?"_
4. _Understand decision process — "Who else is involved?"_
5. _Test timeline — "Is there a forcing function?"_
6. _(optional) Competitive — "Are you evaluating alternatives?"_
7. _(optional) Next step anchor — "If this resonated, what would make sense as a next step?"_

---

## What success looks like

_By the end of this call, we will have:_
- [ ] Confirmed / denied the primary hypothesis
- [ ] Identified the economic buyer
- [ ] Agreed a specific next step with a date
