# Skills Catalogue

72 skills across 7 groups covering the full sales lifecycle.

---

## Group 1 — Find Buyers and Accounts

| # | Skill | Description |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | ICP Builder | Convert an ideal-customer hypothesis into explicit firmographic, technographic, situational, and exclusion criteria |
| 2 | Account Search Planner | Define research queries and source strategy for finding target accounts |
| 3 | Account Fit Scoring | Score account fit with a transparent rubric and sourced evidence |
| 4 | Buyer Committee Mapper | Map likely roles and buying influence from sourced information only |
| 5 | Contact Research Brief | Structure lawful, source-based research on relevant professional contacts |
| 6 | Contact Verification | Assess recency and confidence of supplied contact data |
| 7 | Buying-Trigger Research | Find evidence of a potential need or buying trigger |
| 8 | Territory Planner | Prioritise accounts by fit, potential, coverage, and capacity |
| 9 | Segment Builder | Create and compare practical account segments |
| 10 | Negative ICP / Disqualification | Identify poor-fit accounts and reasons to deprioritise |
