# Golden Test Case 08 — Forecast Challenge

**Test ID:** golden-08  
**Skill:** `skills/05-sales-enablement/forecast-challenge/SKILL.md`  
**Date created:** 2026-10-20

---

## Input

> Challenge this quarterly forecast:
>
> AE claims: "We will close €400K this quarter. We have 4 deals committed: Vantage (€95K), Meridian (€120K), Pinnacle (€60K), and Coastal (€125K)."
>
> Evidence provided:
> - Vantage: In legal review, confirmed by legal contact, close date confirmed. (Well-evidenced)
> - Meridian: Proposal sent 21 days ago, no response. Close date is AE estimate. (Unsupported)
> - Pinnacle: Demo scheduled next week. No discovery notes provided. Budget not confirmed. (Unsupported)
> - Coastal: AE says "should close." No next step documented. Close date 5 days away. (Unsupported)

---

## Expected output

1. **Per-deal assessment** — evidence for/against each "committed" deal
2. **Revised forecast** — what is actually supported by evidence vs. what is assumed
3. **Recommendations** — what each AE needs to do to validate their claim
4. **Risk summary** — top risks to the quarter

Expected key findings:
- Only Vantage is genuinely committed (evidenced)
- Meridian, Pinnacle, Coastal should be "best case" at most, not committed
- Revised committed: €95K; best case: up to €400K if all others close (uncertain)
