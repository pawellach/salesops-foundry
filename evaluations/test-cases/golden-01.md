# Golden Test Case 01 — ICP Builder

**Test ID:** golden-01  
**Skill:** `skills/01-find-buyers/icp-builder/SKILL.md`  
**Date created:** 2026-10-20

---

## Input

> Use the ICP Builder skill. Here is the hypothesis:
>
> "Our best-fit customers are mid-market logistics companies (200–1,000 employees) in Europe that manage more than 10 carriers and currently use a manual (Excel/email) reconciliation process. They typically have a VP Operations or Head of Logistics who is the champion, and finance or the CFO controls the budget."

---

## Expected output

The skill should produce:
1. **Firmographic criteria** — size range, geography, industry, revenue estimate
2. **Technographic criteria** — current stack signals, tools to search for
3. **Situational / trigger criteria** — what buying triggers to look for
4. **Exclusion criteria** — who explicitly does NOT fit
5. **Persona map** — champion role, economic buyer role
6. **ICP confidence note** — labels this as hypothesis, recommends validation cadence

The output must NOT:
- Invent specific companies matching the ICP
- Claim specific market sizes (e.g. "there are 1,200 such companies in Europe") without a source
- Present inferences as verified facts
