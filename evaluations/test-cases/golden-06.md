# Golden Test Case 06 — Discovery Call Plan

**Test ID:** golden-06  
**Skill:** `skills/04-discovery-and-deals/discovery-call-plan/SKILL.md`  
**Date created:** 2026-10-20

---

## Input

> Build a discovery call plan for a 45-minute call with Jordan Rivera at Northlight Technologies.
>
> Known pain (stated in blog post): "Managing data from 30 different carriers — each with their own invoice format — is consuming significant manual effort from our ops team."
>
> Unknown: Budget, timeline, decision process, other stakeholders.

---

## Expected output

A call plan (matching `templates/call-plan.md`) with:
1. **Primary objective** — one sentence, specific to this account
2. **Hypotheses to test** — 2–3 derived from the known pain
3. **Key questions** — 5–7, not generic discovery questions but tailored to Northlight's situation
4. **Success criteria** — specific and measurable
5. **Risks** — at least one (e.g. "Economic buyer may not be on the call")
6. **What NOT to do** — do not present a solution before confirming the pain still exists
