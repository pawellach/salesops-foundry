# Golden Test Case 04 — First-Touch Draft

**Test ID:** golden-04  
**Skill:** `skills/03-run-the-pipeline/first-touch-draft/SKILL.md`  
**Date created:** 2026-10-20

---

## Input

> Draft a first-touch email for Jordan Rivera, VP Operations at Northlight Technologies.
>
> Use the account brief from golden-03. The main angle: their blog post about scaling from 12 to 30 carriers.

---

## Expected output

A draft email (matching `templates/follow-up-email.md`) that:
1. References the specific blog post, not a generic pain statement
2. Asks a question rather than making claims about their internal situation
3. Proposes a small, clear ask (20-minute call)
4. Is clearly marked **DRAFT — human approval required before sending**
5. Does NOT claim specific outcomes ("we can save you X%")
6. Does NOT include invented contact details
