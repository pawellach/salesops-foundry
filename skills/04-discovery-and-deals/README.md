# Group 4 — Discovery, Demos, and Deal Progression

Skills for preparing and running discovery calls, building demos that land, and closing deals with a clear shared plan.

| Skill | What it does for you |
|---|---|
| [Discovery Question Planner](discovery-question-planner/SKILL.md) | Build a question plan tailored to this specific account — not a generic discovery checklist |
| [Discovery Call Plan](discovery-call-plan/SKILL.md) | Map your call objectives, hypotheses, and time allocation before every discovery |
| [Call Notes Structurer](call-notes-structurer/SKILL.md) | Turn raw notes or a transcript into a clean, structured call record |
| [Pain and Impact Mapper](pain-and-impact-mapper/SKILL.md) | Separate what the customer said from what you inferred — keeps your analysis honest |
| [Qualification Framework Mapper](qualification-framework-mapper/SKILL.md) | Work through MEDDICC, SPICED, or BANT with the facts you have — and flag what is missing |
| [Objection Classifier](objection-classifier/SKILL.md) | Identify what type of objection you are dealing with before you respond |
| [Objection Handling Coach](objection-handling-coach/SKILL.md) | Prepare honest, evidence-based responses to the objections you will face |
| [Demo Storyline Builder](demo-storyline-builder/SKILL.md) | Build a demo narrative connected to confirmed needs — not a feature tour |
| [Demo Readiness Review](demo-readiness-review/SKILL.md) | Check whether you have everything you need before the demo — not after |
| [Mutual Action Plan](mutual-action-plan/SKILL.md) | Agree a shared timeline with your prospect — milestones, owners, and next steps |
| [Proposal Outline](proposal-outline/SKILL.md) | Structure a proposal that maps directly to the requirements you have actually verified |
| [Negotiation Planner](negotiation-planner/SKILL.md) | Prepare your options and trade-offs before entering negotiation — know your walk-away |
