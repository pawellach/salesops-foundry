# ROI / Time-Saved Estimator

## Purpose

Produce a transparent, assumption-explicit estimate of the time saved or ROI generated by adopting a new sales tool, skill, or process. The output surfaces every assumption for human review — it does not produce a single authoritative ROI figure.

**This skill cannot guarantee any return on investment or sales improvement. All outputs are directional estimates only.**

## When NOT to use

- When the intent is to produce a certified financial projection (requires a finance professional)
- When no baseline data or estimates are available
- When the goal is to justify a decision already made (this skill is for exploration, not post-hoc rationalisation)

## Required inputs

- `change_description`: what tool, skill, or process is being adopted
- `team_context`: team size, roles affected, and a rough baseline time or metric (even an estimate)

## Optional inputs

- `baseline_metric`: current measured or estimated performance (e.g., "reps spend 3 hours/week on research")
- `expected_improvement_pct`: the team's estimate of improvement (labelled as a hypothesis)
- `time_horizon_weeks`: how many weeks to project over (default: 12)
- `rep_cost_per_hour`: estimated fully-loaded cost per rep per hour (for cost savings calculation)

## Step-by-step procedure

1. Identify what is changing: time, quality, volume, or speed.
2. Establish a baseline: what is the current state? (If unknown, note this and use a conservative placeholder.)
3. State the improvement hypothesis: what the team believes will change, and why.
4. Calculate three scenarios: conservative (50% of expected improvement), base (100%), optimistic (150%).
5. If `rep_cost_per_hour` is supplied, calculate cost savings in each scenario.
6. List every assumption explicitly. Challenge each one: "What would have to be true for this assumption to hold?"
7. State clearly: "These are estimates, not guarantees. Actual results depend on adoption, execution, team context, and factors not modelled here."

## Output contract

```
ROI / TIME-SAVED ESTIMATE — SalesOps Foundry Discovery Skills
Team: 4 SDRs, 12-week horizon

CHANGE: Replacing ad-hoc discovery prep with structured discovery-call-plan skill
GOAL: Reduce prep time and improve call quality

BASELINE (ESTIMATE — not measured):
- Current: reps spend ~45 minutes preparing for each discovery call
- Source: manager estimate (not measured)
- ASSUMPTION A: 45 min is accurate ± 15 min

IMPROVEMENT HYPOTHESIS: structured prep reduces time to 25 min and improves post-call qualification rate
- ASSUMPTION B: 20-minute time saving per call is achievable after 2-week learning curve
- ASSUMPTION C: 3 discovery calls per rep per week average
- ASSUMPTION D: quality improvement leads to 10% improvement in SQL rate (unvalidated)

SCENARIO ANALYSIS (time saved over 12 weeks, 4 reps):
| Scenario | Time saving/call | Calls/week | Total time saved |
|---|---|---|---|
| Conservative (50%) | 10 min | 3 | 24 hours |
| Base (100%) | 20 min | 3 | 48 hours |
| Optimistic (150%) | 30 min | 3 | 72 hours |

COST SAVINGS (if rep_cost_per_hour = $80):
| Scenario | Cost saving |
|---|---|
| Conservative | $1,920 |
| Base | $3,840 |
| Optimistic | $5,760 |

REVENUE IMPACT: Not modelled — the link between prep quality and SQL rate improvement (Assumption D) is unvalidated. Run a sales experiment (see Sales Experiment Designer) to measure this before projecting revenue impact.

ASSUMPTIONS SUMMARY:
A — Baseline time estimate is from memory, not measurement. Measure before starting.
B — Learning curve not modelled; savings may not appear until Week 3.
C — Call volume varies by rep and season.
D — SQL rate improvement is a hypothesis only.

IMPORTANT: These are directional estimates only. This skill cannot guarantee any result. Actual savings depend on adoption quality, rep execution, and factors not included in this model.
```

## Evidence and citation rules

- Every number in the output must trace to a supplied input or a labelled assumption.
- Do not cite industry benchmark ROI figures without a verified, named source.
- The disclaimer ("These are directional estimates only") must appear in every output.

## Confidence/uncertainty handling

- If baseline is not measured: use "ESTIMATE" labels throughout and recommend a measurement period before projecting.
- Never present a single "expected ROI" as certain.

## Safety, privacy, and compliance constraints

- Do not use this output in a vendor proposal or external sales pitch without independent financial review.

## Human approval requirements

**This skill produces an estimation document for internal planning.** A manager or finance owner reviews before using in any decision, budget request, or external communication.

## Failure modes and fallback behaviour

- No baseline supplied: note the limitation and produce a framework with placeholder assumptions clearly marked.

## Acceptance criteria

- Three scenarios present (conservative, base, optimistic).
- Every assumption listed with a challenge question.
- Disclaimer present in output.
- No single guaranteed ROI figure stated.

## Evaluation cases

**Case 1 — Full inputs supplied**
Input: change_description="structured discovery skill", team_context="4 SDRs", baseline_metric="45 min/call", expected_improvement_pct=44, rep_cost_per_hour=80, time_horizon_weeks=12. Expected: all 3 scenarios with time and cost savings, assumptions section with 4 items, disclaimer present.

**Case 2 — No baseline**
Input: change_description="new pipeline tracking", team_context="5 reps, no current process". Expected: all assumptions labelled as placeholders, strong recommendation to measure baseline first, disclaimer prominently placed.
