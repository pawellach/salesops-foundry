# Group 7 — Quality, Safety, and Governance

Skills for verifying AI output before it goes out — catching hallucinations, protecting data, and ensuring human control over consequential actions.

| Skill | What it does for you |
|---|---|
| [Source and Citation Auditor](source-citation-auditor/SKILL.md) | Check whether claims in AI-generated content are backed by actual evidence |
| [Hallucination Challenge](hallucination-challenge/SKILL.md) | Hunt for invented facts, dates, people, and metrics before anything goes out |
| [Prompt-Injection Defence Review](prompt-injection-defence/SKILL.md) | Check whether web pages, documents, or CRM text could hijack AI instructions |
| [Privacy and Data-Minimisation Review](privacy-data-minimisation-review/SKILL.md) | Reduce unnecessary personal and customer data in your AI outputs |
| [Outreach Compliance Checklist](outreach-compliance-checklist/SKILL.md) | Flag consent, opt-out, and jurisdiction questions before sending at scale |
| [Human Approval Gate](human-approval-gate/SKILL.md) | Classify any AI action by risk level — and route it to the right approval tier |
| [Model/Cost Router](model-cost-router/SKILL.md) | Route work to the right model for the job and track the rationale |
| [Regression Evaluator](regression-evaluator/SKILL.md) | Re-run quality tests after any changes to verify nothing got worse |
