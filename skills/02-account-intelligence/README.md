# Group 2 — Account Intelligence and Signals

Skills for researching target accounts and spotting signals that indicate a buying window is opening.

| Skill | What it does for you |
|---|---|
| [Account Brief Builder](account-brief-builder/SKILL.md) | Build a one-page brief on any target account — sourced facts, not assumptions |
| [Company Change Monitor](company-change-monitor/SKILL.md) | Track relevant company changes that signal a buying window is opening |
| [Job-Change Signal](job-change-signal/SKILL.md) | Spot role changes at target accounts that often precede buying decisions |
| [Hiring Signal](hiring-signal/SKILL.md) | Read hiring patterns to infer where a company is investing and what problems they are solving |
| [Funding-Round Signal](funding-round-signal/SKILL.md) | Assess what a funding announcement means for your timing and pitch angle |
| [Product/Technology Signal](product-technology-signal/SKILL.md) | Understand what a company's product moves reveal about their current priorities |
| [Competitor Engagement](competitor-engagement/SKILL.md) | Summarise what competitors are doing publicly — without overstepping on data access |
| [Content Engagement Signal](content-engagement-signal/SKILL.md) | Use authorised engagement data to gauge interest before reaching out |
| [Intent Signal Aggregator](intent-signal-aggregator/SKILL.md) | Combine multiple signals into one prioritised, confidence-weighted picture |
| [Signal-to-Action Prioritiser](signal-to-action-prioritiser/SKILL.md) | Decide what to do next based on signals — with a human approving the action |
