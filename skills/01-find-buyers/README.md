# Group 1 — Find Buyers and Accounts

Skills for defining who is worth pursuing and building a qualified target list before any outreach begins.

| Skill | What it does for you |
|---|---|
| [ICP Builder](icp-builder/SKILL.md) | Define exactly which companies are worth your time — firmographics, fit signals, and hard exclusions |
| [Account Search Planner](account-search-planner/SKILL.md) | Plan where and how to find the right accounts before you start searching |
| [Account Fit Scoring](account-fit-scoring/SKILL.md) | Score any account against your ICP with clear evidence — not gut feel |
| [Buyer Committee Mapper](buyer-committee-mapper/SKILL.md) | Map who is involved in the buying decision before your first call |
| [Contact Research Brief](contact-research-brief/SKILL.md) | Build a professional profile of a contact using only public, permitted sources |
| [Contact Verification](contact-verification/SKILL.md) | Check whether contact data is still current before you reach out |
| [Buying-Trigger Research](buying-trigger-research/SKILL.md) | Find evidence that a company might be ready to buy right now |
| [Territory Planner](territory-planner/SKILL.md) | Prioritise which accounts to work this quarter — by fit, potential, and your capacity |
| [Segment Builder](segment-builder/SKILL.md) | Group accounts into actionable segments for focused, targeted outreach |
| [Negative ICP / Disqualification](negative-icp/SKILL.md) | Identify accounts that look good on paper but will drain your pipeline — cut them early |
