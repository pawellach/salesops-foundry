# Group 5 — Sales Enablement and Management

Skills for coaching reps, reviewing deals, managing pipeline health, and running a data-driven sales team.

| Skill | What it does for you |
|---|---|
| [Founder-Led Sales Coach](founder-led-sales-coach/SKILL.md) | Lightweight sales guidance for founders doing early-stage selling without a full team |
| [Sales Call Coach](sales-call-coach/SKILL.md) | Get feedback on your calls based on what actually happened — not generic advice |
| [Deal Review Facilitator](deal-review-facilitator/SKILL.md) | Stress-test any deal with the right questions — without inventing deal health |
| [Pipeline Hygiene Audit](pipeline-hygiene-audit/SKILL.md) | Find stale, inconsistent, or missing data in your pipeline before it costs you a forecast |
| [Weekly Pipeline Report](weekly-pipeline-report/SKILL.md) | Summarise what changed this week, what is at risk, and what needs your attention |
| [Forecast Challenge](forecast-challenge/SKILL.md) | Challenge your own forecast with evidence and scenario analysis — before your manager does |
| [Sales Manager 1:1 Prep](sales-manager-1on1-prep/SKILL.md) | Structure coaching conversations around facts and goals, not impressions |
| [Win/Loss Review](win-loss-review/SKILL.md) | Learn what actually drove a win or loss — documented reasons, not post-hoc stories |
| [Sales Experiment Designer](sales-experiment-designer/SKILL.md) | Design a measurable test to validate a sales hypothesis — with a baseline and stop rules |
| [Enablement Content Builder](enablement-content-builder/SKILL.md) | Create reusable sales guidance from approved source material — for the whole team |
