**Prepared by:** Account Intelligence Agent / Hakken

**Product:** Acme Carrier Connect (fictional carrier invoice reconciliation SaaS)

## Ideal Customer Profile

### Firmographic criteria

| Criterion | Target | Exclusion |
|---|---|---|
| Industry | Logistics, freight forwarding, 3PL, e-commerce fulfilment | Pure retail, manufacturing without own logistics |
| Employee count | 100–1,000 | < 50 (too small for enterprise deal), > 5,000 (different procurement cycle) |
| Annual revenue | $10M–$200M | < $5M (unlikely to budget), > $500M (requires different product tier) |
| Geography | EU, UK, Nordics (MVP) | APAC, LATAM (out of scope for Year 1) |
| HQ | Major logistics hubs preferred | |

### Technographic criteria

| Criterion | Ideal signal | Source type |
|---|---|---|
| ERP system | SAP S/4HANA, Oracle NetSuite, Microsoft Dynamics | LinkedIn jobs, job ads |
| Current reconciliation method | Manual (Excel, email) | Job descriptions mentioning "manual reconciliation" |
| CRM | Salesforce or HubSpot | LinkedIn jobs |

### Situational criteria (buying triggers)

| Trigger | Relevance | How to detect |
|---|---|---|
| Growing carrier network (>10 carriers) | High — complexity drives reconciliation pain | Hiring signals, press releases |
| Recent funding round (Series A–C) | Medium — budget available, scaling pressure | Crunchbase, company blog |
| New VP Operations / Head of Logistics hired | High — new leader often reviews vendor stack | LinkedIn job change alerts |
| Compliance / audit pressure mentioned | High — reconciliation errors become a liability | Press, earnings calls |

### Exclusion criteria

- Companies with a dedicated in-house engineering team building carrier integrations
- Companies currently mid-contract with a direct competitor (check signals before outreach)
- Companies in active M&A process (decision-making frozen)

This ICP is based on Acme Corp's internal win/loss review (fictional) and 6 months of deal data. All criteria are hypotheses — validate against actual deal outcomes quarterly.
