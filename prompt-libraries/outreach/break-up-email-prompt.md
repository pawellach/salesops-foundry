# Break-up Email Prompt

**Purpose:** Draft a final "break-up" email that closes the sequence gracefully and leaves the door open.

**Inputs:**
- `{{prospect_name}}` — first name
- `{{company_name}}` — their company
- `{{number_of_prior_touches}}` — how many times you've reached out

---

## Prompt template

```
Write a break-up email to {{prospect_name}} at {{company_name}} after {{number_of_prior_touches}} unanswered messages.

Rules:
- 2 sentences maximum
- Acknowledge you won't follow up again
- Leave the door open for them to reach out when timing is right
- No guilt-tripping, no passive aggression, no "I guess this isn't a priority for you"
- Optional: one very light final ask (not required)
- Tone: respectful, brief, human

Format:
[email body only]
```

---

## Usage notes

- The break-up email has a surprisingly high response rate — prospects who were busy often respond to a graceful close.
- Do not use this as a manipulation tactic ("reverse psychology"). Use it because you genuinely intend to stop following up.
- After a break-up email, stop. If they respond, re-engage. If they don't, move on.
